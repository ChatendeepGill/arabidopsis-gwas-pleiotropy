**Purpose**:
Test whether GWAS detectability is associated with representation in the co-expression network or with gene tissue specificity. 

Two analyses are performed:
1. A Fisher’s exact test comparing the frequency of GWAS hits among genes inside and outside the co-expression network
2. A binomial logistic regression testing whether tissue specificity predicts whether a gene has at least one GWAS hit

**Inputs:**
- `gene_trait_table.csv`: Generated by `02_build_gene_trait_effects.R`. Genes in this file are treated as having at least one retained GWAS association
- `data/external/coexpression/arabidopsis_coexpression_metrics.rds`: Gene-level co-expression network statistics included in this repository
- `data/external/tissue_specificity/arabidopsis_tissue_specificity_tau.rds`: Gene-level tissue-specificity estimates included in this repository
- `Arabidopsis_thaliana.TAIR10.pep.all.fa`: TAIR10 protein FASTA used to define the background protein-coding gene set. This file was obtained from Ensembl Plants and is not included in the repository. 

**Analyses:**

**Co-expression network representation:** TAIR10 protein-coding genes are classified according to whether they are represented in the co-expression network, and whether they have at least one retained GWAS association. Fisher’s exact test is used to compare GWAS-hit frequencies between genes inside and outside the co-expression network. The script also reports the proportion of genes with a GWAS hit in each group.

**Tissue specificity:** Genes in the tissue-specificity dataset are classified according to whether they have at least one retained GWAS association. A binomial logistic-regression model is fitted: *GWAS hit status ~ tau_mean*. The script reports: logistic-regression coefficients, mean tau among genes with and without a GWAS hit, odds ratio, and 95% confidence intervals for the odds ratios.

**Outputs:** 
Statistical results are printed to the R console. No additional results files are saved.

**Notes:**
- The TAIR10 protein FASTA is used to define the background gene set for the co-expression network enrichment test
- Lower tau values indicate broader expression across tissues, while higher tau values indicate greater tissue specificity
- GWAS-hit status indicates that a gene has at least one association retained by the preceding processing steps
